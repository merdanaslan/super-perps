## Checked details

- **Brand:** two continuous brackets, graphite/red/gold, matching the selected
  sculptural hero. Only the transparent logo layer floats by a few pixels with a
  slight rotation over 6.5 seconds; the pixel-wave base stays fixed. Reduced motion
  disables the animation. Asset provenance is in
  [brand-assets.md](../brand-assets.md).
- **Theme:** black `#000`, charcoal `#101114`/`#17191f`, white auth controls, green
  `#00c78b`, red `#ff3d65`, gold focus treatment. No blue interface accents. Actual
  coin logos retain their native colors. Green/red filled actions use dark text.
- **Home:** profile/search, original hero, direct signup/login, actual market
  overview and educational content. The redundant Live badge is removed.
  Search no longer redirects to Markets.
  The footer pairs the zupa wordmark with a linked "Powered by Phoenix" attribution
  and Phoenix's official orange mark.
- **Search:** full-page back and autofocus, clear action, name/symbol matching,
  ranked discovery and explicit empty results. The redundant context row beneath
  the suggestion tabs is removed. Selecting ETH opens its trade tab.
- **Markets:** horizontally scrollable categories and aligned market/price/change
  rows. Stocks displays venue-supplied contracts/logos; classifications are backed
  by metadata plus maintained overrides, not fabricated listings. Both redundant
  Perpetuals badges are removed; the quote unit remains visible.
- **Account:** welcome and direct auth actions; settings visible without another
  Settings sheet. About contains the repository placeholder and project notices.
  Added space below the welcome subtitle after visual review.
- **Assets:** Title-only header with automatic balance refresh. Unknown or
  failed balances remain dashes. Deposit/Withdraw open their corresponding tabs.
- **Trade:** neutral Open/Close segment, green/red Long/Short, Market/Limit,
  base/USDC amount, percentage slider, TP/SL fields, genuine order book, real
  positions/orders/conditional tabs and expandable candlesticks. The slider has a
  36px hit area around its 4px track. Capacity stays unavailable while logged out.
  The submit action has 20px clearance below estimates. The account refresh button
  and healthy Live book label are removed; automatic account updates and error
  retry/reconnect controls remain.
  Time in force sits beside TP/SL: GTC for limits, FOK for market orders, with a
  tappable explanation. The 0.5% footer line is removed while the execution bound
  and review price limit remain.
- **Navigation:** flat full-width bottom bar. At 375×812 and 390×844 there is no
  horizontal document overflow and the expanded chart/nav remain within bounds.
- **Auth:** clicking Sign up / Log in opens the real Privy modal in one step. Its
  email input is focusable and it closes correctly. No new login or signature was
  performed by the agent.
- **Missing settings:** sign-in unavailability is explained beside its disabled
  action. Public browsing remains available with blank provider identifiers.

## Functional findings resolved

1. Preserve canonical market casing (`kBONK`, `kPEPE`, `kSHIB`) on route reload.
2. Keep unknown/stale account values unknown instead of showing artificial zeroes.
3. Move shared trading context into its own module to avoid fast-refresh identity
   errors when provider and consumers change together.
4. Preserve source module execution order in the configured production build to
   prevent the shared Solana codec initialization failure found in the final check.
5. Use a local RPC proxy for development/preview after the shared public endpoint
   rejected browser-origin requests; hosted installations require their own RPC.
   The creator's subsequently supplied Helius endpoint is configured locally and
   browser balance reads have been verified against it.
